Life on the Island

Taniti is the kind of place where time forgets to move forward. Palm trees lean like they’re tired of standing, the air smells like salt and grilled fish, and nobody is in a rush unless it’s to catch the last ferry or the best sunset spot. Locals live simply. Morning markets, afternoon naps, and long conversations over coffee are standard operating procedure. If you’re looking for chaos, this is the wrong island. If you want peace, welcome home. Hiking trails cut through dense jungle, opening up to cliffside views that feel illegal to see for free. At night, the sky actually has stars. Real ones. Not the sad city knockoffs. Power outages happen, but everyone just shrugs and lights candles like it’s 1850 again.
