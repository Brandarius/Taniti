Quick Answers

Best time to visit?

November to April is dry and sunny. May to October is greener and less crowded. Both are fine, honestly.

Need a visa?

Most people can stay 90 days without one. Check with your embassy to be sure.

What currency?

Taniti Dollars, but US Dollars work everywhere. Credit cards are fine at most places.

Family-friendly?

Yeah. Beaches, museums, activities for kids. It's not just for couples on honeymoon.

What language?

English works. Locals speak Tanitian but everyone in tourism speaks English.

Getting around?

Rent a car, take a taxi, ride a bus, bike it. Whatever works for you.
