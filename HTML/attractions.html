Things to Do (Or Not Do)

What's Worth Your Time

We kept it simple. Here's what actually matters.

Active Volcano

It's a literal active volcano. You can hike near it if you're into that. Guides make sure you don't fall into lava. Decent views, minimal death risk.

Yellow Leaf Bay

White sand, calm water, zero waves trying to murder you. Bring a book. Forget the book exists. Stare at the ocean instead.

Rainforest Trails

Trails go through actual jungle. Humid. Buggy. Beautiful. You'll sweat a lot and then see something incredible and forget you were complaining.

Snorkeling

The reef is absurdly colorful. Fish don't care that you're there. Water is so clear you'll question if it's real. Spoiler: it is.

Local History Museum

Small but legit. You'll learn about the island's indigenous roots and wonder why this place isn't more famous. Worth an hour.

Merriton Landing Nightlife

Clubs, bars, restaurants, and that one guy playing guitar who's either really good or you're too tipsy to tell. If you want nightlife, this is the spot.

Where to Go

Taniti City

The main hub. Beaches that don't quit, shops selling things you don't need but will buy anyway, and historic buildings that look great in photos. Most tours start here.

Merriton Landing

This is where the party is. If you want nightlife, this is the spot. If you don't, avoid it after 10 PM.
